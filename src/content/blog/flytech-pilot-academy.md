## Meet FlyTech Pilot Academy: A Flight School Built for the Modern Era

If you haven't heard of FlyTech Pilot Academy yet, you're going to want to. Based out of Saint Joseph, Missouri (KSTJ), FlyTech is positioning itself to be a next-gen flight school — combining modern aircraft, integrated digital tools, simulator training, and a flexible, student-centric experience.

Here are a few things that set FlyTech apart:

- Their fleet includes the **Sling NGT** (a modern, technologically advanced aircraft) alongside Cessna 172s, allowing consistent exposure to glass-cockpit avionics.
- They use a full-motion certified simulator (Redbird MCX) for instrument and procedural training, saving costs and keeping students flying (even when weather intervenes).
- From sport pilot certification all the way to CFI / instrument instructor ratings, they offer a complete training ladder.
- They emphasize accessibility and flexibility — you can train part-time or more aggressively, and they help accommodate scheduling.
- They're committed to keeping training affordable through efficient operations and smart equipment choices.

FlyTech is more than a flight school — they're a vision for where flight training _should_ go: blending technology, affordability, and student-first operations.

## Why the Partnership Makes Sense

When we first talked with FlyTech, it was almost electric — there was an alignment in philosophy. At Right Rudder Marketing, our mission is clear: to help flight schools scale sustainably, bring in more students, and build strong, resilient operations.

Here's why FlyTech and Right Rudder are a good match:

**Shared Vision of Innovation**  
FlyTech isn't doing "training like it's always been done." They're forward-looking, and they see how aviation — especially general aviation training — has to evolve. That hunger for innovation is exactly what we love working with.

**Marketing That Understands the Industry**  
One of the biggest failures I see in our space is when a "general" marketing agency tries to work with a flight school. They don't understand the metrics, the cadences, the regulatory challenges, the student journey. At Right Rudder, we _live and breathe_ aviation marketing. FlyTech needed a partner that could speak pilot, not marketer — that's us.

**Scalable Growth, Not Just Leads**  
It's not enough to bring in leads. We want to convert them, nurture them, scale their customer lifetime value, and help FlyTech build a sustainable fly-training pipeline. This includes SEO, PPC, content, conversion optimization, reputation, funnel design, and more.

**Addressing the Pilot Shortage**  
The aviation industry is facing a critical pilot supply crunch. Flight schools that can attract, train, and graduate pilots efficiently will be vital to filling that gap. By combining FlyTech's efficient training model with top-tier marketing, we aim to grow throughput (without sacrificing quality).
