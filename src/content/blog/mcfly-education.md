We're excited to welcome **McFly Education** as the newest partner of Right Rudder Marketing.

## Who Is McFly Education?

McFly Education is a dynamic flight training organization based at **Cable Airport (KCCB)** in Upland, California. Their mission is simple: provide a personalized, safe, and results-driven flight training experience for every student. Whether you’re pursuing a Private Pilot License or working toward a career in aviation, McFly Education offers a flexible and focused training environment that meets you where you are.

With a commitment to mentorship, modern instruction, and hands-on experience, McFly is building a name for itself in Southern California aviation.
