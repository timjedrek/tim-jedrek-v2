[**Right Rudder Marketing**](https://rightruddermarketing.com) is thrilled to announce our strategic partnership with Universal Flight Training (UFT), one of Florida's premier aviation education institutions. Based at Sarasota–Bradenton International Airport (SRQ), UFT has established itself as a leader in pilot training excellence, and we're honored to support their continued growth and success.

## A Legacy Built on Excellence and Results

What began in 2010 as a modest operation with one aircraft and a small office has evolved into a thriving flight academy under the leadership of founder and retired USMC Master Gunnery Sergeant José Santos. UFT's track record speaks volumes: over 500 graduates and an exceptional 98% checkride pass rate. This success reflects their unwavering commitment to rigorous instruction, experienced educators, and a philosophy that puts student success first.

## Comprehensive Training for Every Aviation Goal

UFT serves pilots at every stage of their journey with a complete range of certification programs:

### **Core Certifications:**

- Private Pilot License (PPL)
- Instrument Rating (IFR)
- Commercial Pilot License
- Multi-Engine Rating
- Certified Flight Instructor (CFI & CFII)

### **Academic Partnerships:**

For students seeking to combine flight training with higher education, UFT's collaboration with Purdue Global provides online bachelor's degree programs in Professional Flight and Aviation Management—perfect for those building comprehensive aviation careers.

## Full-Service Aviation Hub

UFT distinguishes itself by offering everything pilots need under one roof:

- **Testing & Certification:** Their on-site FAA CATS Testing Center (code: LAS34204) enables students to complete critical certification exams without leaving the facility.

- **Aircraft Maintenance:** Expert maintenance services include comprehensive inspections, tire replacements, and AD compliance, handled by experienced technicians Paul and Frank who ensure every aircraft meets the highest safety standards.

- **Modern Fleet:** Students train on a sophisticated fleet featuring Garmin G1000-equipped Cessna 172s, PA-28 Archers, and PA-44 Seminoles, providing hands-on experience with cutting-edge avionics technology.

## Leadership That Inspires Excellence

UFT's success stems from exceptional leadership and instruction. CEO José Santos, Chief Flight Instructor Joe Vasquez, and their team of Certified Flight Instructors bring approximately 30,000 hours of collective flight experience to the school. Their diverse military and civilian backgrounds provide students with mentorship that goes far beyond textbook knowledge.

## Strategically Located on Florida's Gulf Coast

Located at 8140 15th St E in Sarasota, UFT serves the broader Gulf Coast region, including Bradenton, Venice, and St. Petersburg. This prime location combines modern instructional facilities with the rich aviation heritage that makes Florida a natural choice for flight training.

## A Partnership Built for Growth

Right Rudder Marketing partners with flight schools that demonstrate both mission-driven excellence and growth potential. UFT exemplifies both qualities through their proven results, comprehensive services, and strategic university partnerships.

Our collaboration will focus on expanding UFT's digital presence, optimizing their search engine visibility, and enhancing lead conversion systems to connect more aspiring pilots with their exceptional training programs. With the ongoing global pilot shortage creating unprecedented opportunities, this partnership arrives at the perfect moment.
