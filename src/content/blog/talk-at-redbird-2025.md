### Speaking at Redbird Migration 2025  

I had the opportunity to speak at Redbird Migration 2025, a key event for the flight training industry. This annual conference brings together flight school owners, CFIs, and aviation educators to discuss the future of pilot training. My session focused on how flight schools can use digital marketing strategies to increase student enrollments.  

### Key Takeaways from My Talk  

Marketing a flight school requires more than just a great training program—it’s about visibility, lead nurturing, and retention. During my session, I covered:  

✅ **Turning Website Visitors into Student Leads** – How a well-optimized website and lead capture system make all the difference.  
✅ **The Role of PPC and SEO** – The benefits of running paid search campaigns alongside an organic content strategy.  
✅ **CRM Automation for Better Follow-Up** – How flight schools can stop losing leads by implementing automated workflows.  
✅ **Common Marketing Mistakes to Avoid** – Missteps I see flight schools make and how to fix them.  
✅ **Real-World Case Studies** – Examples of schools that have grown their enrollments using these strategies.
