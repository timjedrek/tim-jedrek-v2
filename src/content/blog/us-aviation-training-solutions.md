## Discover the Gold Standard in Aviation Training

For more than 30 years, US Aviation Training Solutions (USATS) has set the benchmark for excellence in aviation education. Based at the Space Coast Regional Airport (KTIX) in Titusville, Florida, USATS is far more than a traditional flight school—it’s a global launchpad for future aviators. With FAA Part 141 certification and a progressive, structured training approach, USATS has proudly trained thousands of pilots representing over 120 countries worldwide.

### A Full-Spectrum Training Hub

USATS distinguishes itself by offering complete training programs for both fixed-wing aircraft and helicopters. Whether a student dreams of flying commercial jets or commanding rotorcraft, USATS has a structured and fast-track training pipeline:

- **Airplane Training**: From Private Pilot License (PPL) to Commercial Pilot License (CPL) and Certified Flight Instructor (CFI/CFII), students progress through streamlined programs under FAA Part 141 regulations.
- **Helicopter Training**: Just as robust as the airplane programs, helicopter training extends to ATP-Rotorcraft, including military-grade instruction and a dedicated professional pilot program.

### Built for Global Reach

USATS is a magnet for international students, thanks to its M-1 visa support, financial aid partnerships, and hands-on student services. With personalized onboarding and support, students from all corners of the world can feel confident and prepared as they embark on their flight training journey in the U.S.

### Fleet, Facilities, and Florida Sunshine

The USATS 38-acre campus boasts over 320 flyable days per year—a weather advantage few locations can match. Their diverse fleet includes glass-cockpit Cessna 172s, the Alto-NG, Robinson R44s, Schweizer S-300s, and even UH-60 helicopters. Students also benefit from access to a mix of coastal, restricted, and Class C/D airspace—ideal for developing real-world piloting skills.

### Elite Instruction Backed by Real-World Experience

USATS leadership reads like a who’s who of military and commercial aviation:

- **Brian Craddock**, CEO and Managing Director, brings deep experience from U.S. Department of State and Army aviation operations.
- **Ray Deptula**, with 3,000+ flight hours and naval aviation background, contributes mission-critical insights to the curriculum.
- **Stephane Rebeix**, Chief Flight Instructor, has logged over 9,000 hours, specializing in helicopters and IFR glass cockpit operations.
