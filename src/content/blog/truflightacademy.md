We are excited to announce that Right Rudder Marketing is now partnering with TruFlight Academy, a helicopter flight school based in North Texas. [TruFlight Academy](https://truflightacademy.com) offers programs for new pilots, fixed-wing pilots transitioning to rotorcraft, and experienced rotorcraft pilots looking to build skills.

## Why TruFlight Academy Stands Out

- TruFlight Academy uses the modern Cabri G2 helicopters for training — they highlight that they were the first U.S. school using this aircraft with advanced avionics.

- They are veteran-owned and women-owned, operating out of Finney Field near Howe and Sherman, Texas.

- Their training programs cover Private Pilot, Instrument, Commercial, Certified Flight Instructor (CFI) for rotorcraft, and add-ons like external load training.

- They offer discovery flights (“try before you commit”), rental options, and even mobile training (they can come to you).

- Safety and instructor experience are key: they note their CFIs typically have 700+ hours, many over 1,000.

## How Right Rudder Marketing Will Help

Our partnership will focus on amplifying TruFlight Academy’s story and offerings to help them reach their target students—those eager to begin a helicopter-pilot career, or fixed-wing pilots seeking rotorcraft add-ons. Key focus areas will include:

- Website strategy and conversion funnel design (discovery flight leads, enrollment).

- Paid-ads and search engine optimisation to drive traffic from Texas and beyond.

- Review generation, social media outreach, and branding to emphasise their modern fleet and veteran/women-owned status.

- Analytics and CRM integration to track student acquisition from lead to enrollment.

- Content marketing (blog posts, case-studies of students, instructor profiles) to build trust and visibility.

## Why This Matters for Students

Choosing the right helicopter school is a key decision for anyone looking at this path. With TruFlight Academy you get:

- A modern training platform (Cabri G2) with advanced avionics that reflect the real world of rotorcraft flying.

- Experienced instructors who bring real-world hours and a passion to teach.

- Flexible options—including on-site housing if you’re traveling in, and mobile training if you live farther from the base.

Clear paths: whether you’re starting fresh or adding on an instrument rating or external-load capability, you’ll see how the programme fits your goal.
