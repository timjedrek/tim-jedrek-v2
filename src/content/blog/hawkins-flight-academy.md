When aviation meets strategy, beautiful things happen. ## Who Is Hawkins Flight Academy?

If you haven't yet encountered them, Hawkins Flight Academy is a flight training institution with campuses in **Shelbyville, Tennessee (KSYI)** and **Tupelo, Mississippi (KTUP)**.

What sets Hawkins apart:

- **Modern, technically advanced aircraft** — They use RV-12s with Garmin G3X glass panels, avionics, and autopilot features to give students relevant, real-world experience
- **Program-based pricing** — Fixed costs per certificate rather than strictly hourly rates, which helps reduce financial surprises
- **Pro Pilot Pathway** — A structured, accelerated plan that takes students from zero to CFI
- **Mentorship-focused instruction** — Their instructors aren't just time-builders, but teachers genuinely invested in student progress

All of this means Hawkins is operating with a future-forward mindset—preparing students not just for their first license, but for a meaningful, career-oriented flight trajectory.
